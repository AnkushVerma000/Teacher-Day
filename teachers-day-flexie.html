The invitation

Respected Professor's, research is a slow kind of devotion — long hours in the lab, patient guidance, believing in an idea before it works. As students building the next generation of flexible electronics, we owe much of that progress to you.

No speeches longer than they need to be, no formalities you have to sit through — just coffee, cake, and a room full of people glad to know you.

— The FlexE Students

In the lab, we work on circuits and sensors that bend without breaking. Today, we simply want to bend the routine and say thank you.
